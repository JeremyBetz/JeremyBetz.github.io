Jeremy Betz

Data science, quantitative research, and soccer analytics.

I’m interested in problems where understanding how the pieces of a system interact matters as much as producing an answer. That interest leads me to football research, public-interest data, and questions about people, incentives, and structures.

Current Work

Moving the Defense

Independent football tracking-data research on localized defensive reorganization. Across three separate tracking environments, outward off-ball movement is associated with more subsequent local reorganization than comparable movement toward goal. An analyst demo makes the measurement and its limits concrete.

Disrupting the Network — Analytics Cup 2.0

Experimental Python toolkit and football research for the PySport Analytics Cup. A protected receiver-ranking benchmark shows that transparent defensive geometry adds information beyond attacking geometry when ranking the provider-targeted receiver.

Chicago Transit Data Observatory

An early-stage API-to-database analytical project focused on Chicago transit reliability.
