### [Moving the Defense](https://github.com/JeremyBetz/moving-the-defense)

**Status: Active research**

An independent football tracking-data project studying the relationship between off-ball attacking movement and localized defensive reorganization. The work brings together multiple tracking-data providers to examine how movement away from the ball may change the defensive picture around it.

### MLS International Roster Spot Valuation Tool

**Status: Completed**

A U.S. Soccer Hackathon finalist project built with Opta data, focused on valuing MLS international roster spots. The project was later presented at the OptaPro Forum in Chicago.

### Analytics Cup 2.0

**Status: Exploratory / in development**

An early research project on defensive positioning from a network perspective. It asks how defenders might disrupt attacking spatial connections while preserving defensive structure; results are not yet available.

### Chicago Transit Data Observatory

**Status: Early-stage development**

An API-to-database analytical project exploring Chicago transit reliability. It is being developed as a foundation for careful, usable analysis rather than as a finished public product.
